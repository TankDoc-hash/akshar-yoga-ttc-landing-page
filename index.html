Sadhana & Purpose

Our sanctuary is created for all who value authentic yogic depth, comfort and care — not only for the body, but for the soul.

Within our serene residential campuses in Bangalore (CSE Residences & Purantha Sanctuary), we cultivate an atmosphere of deep quietude and mindfulness. Fresh nature, disciplined daily sadhana, and wholesome Sattvik meals create the optimal foundation for profound inner change.

In classical yogic tradition, a 40-day mandala represents the biological and neurological cycle required to recalibrate the nervous system, instill enduring habits, and master the pedagogical confidence needed to teach globally.

200-Hour Comprehensive Syllabus

Full TTC Curriculum Breakdown

A systematic progression across practical execution, subtle energetics, and pedagogical mastery.

Module 1: Foundations of Asana & Alignment

60 Hours

Foundational postures, standing, seated, twisting, backbends, and inversions. Emphasis on classical alignment, muscle activation, contraindications, and hands-on adjustments.

Surya Namaskar Variations Standing & Balancing Asanas Spinal Alignment & Joints Hands-on Adjustments

Module 2: Pranayama, Mudras & Subtle Energy

35 Hours

Mastery of traditional breathwork, nadi purification, neuromuscular energy locks (Bandhas), and hand gestures (Hastamudras) for channelizing vital life force.

Nadi Shodhana & Kapalabhati Bhastrika & Ujjayi Hastamudras Mula & Uddiyana Bandhas

Module 3: Yoga Anatomy & Physiology

25 Hours

Practical human anatomy for yoga teachers: musculoskeletal dynamics, respiratory mechanics, nervous system regulation, fascial lines, and injury prevention protocols.

Skeletal & Muscular Systems Respiratory Mechanics Autonomic Nervous System Injury Prevention

Module 4: Yogic Philosophy & Ethics

30 Hours

Deep study of Patanjali's Yoga Sutras, the Eight Limbs of Ashtanga Yoga, Yamas & Niyamas, classical Himalayan Siddha traditions, and contemporary teacher ethics.

Patanjali Yoga Sutras Eight Limbs (Ashtanga) Yamas & Niyamas Teacher Ethics & Sadhana

Module 5: Teaching Methodology & Sequencing

35 Hours

Principles of intelligent class sequencing, verbal cueing precision, voice modulation, adjustments, classroom psychology, and catering to beginner vs advanced students.

Intelligent Sequencing Verbal Cues & Voice Classroom Management Student Modifications

Module 6: Practicum & Teaching Assessment

15 Hours

Supervised student teaching sessions, hands-on peer instruction, detailed faculty feedback, and practical certification examination.

Live Student Teaching Peer Group Practicum Faculty Feedback Sessions Certification Exam
