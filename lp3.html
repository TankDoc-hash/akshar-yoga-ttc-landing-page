03 // ACADEMIC RIGOR & PRACTICE

Complete 200-Hour Accredited Syllabus & Daily Rhythm.

Ancient oral lineage paired with modern biomechanical precision. Certified by Yoga Alliance USA and the Yoga Certification Board (YCB), Government of India.

PILLAR 01 // 100 HOURS

Classical Asana Mastery & Biomechanics

100 Practical Hours • 84 Postures

Rigorous immersion into 84 classical postures, alignment geometry, and muscular stabilization. Seekers transition from gross effort to effortless posture stillness (Sthira Sukham Asanam).

  • 84 Classical Postures: Inversions, arm balances, deep hip openers, twists, and standing balance mechanics.
  • Skeletal Geometries: Drishti (gaze points), bandha engagement, and pelvic girdle stabilization.
  • Namaskar Variations: Akshar Surya Namaskar, Chandra Namaskar, and dynamic vinyasa flow progressions.
PILLAR 02 // 30 HOURS

Pranayama, Mudras & Subtle Energy

30 Intensive Hours • Breath Mastery

Mastery of life force regulation through ancient Himalayan breathwork. Dissect subtle Nadis, Chakras, and Kundalini activation principles under experienced lineage acharyas.

  • Kumbhaka Science: Antara (internal) and Bahya (external) breath retentions with pulse control.
  • Nadi & Chakra Dynamics: Purification of Ida, Pingala, and Sushumna channels for spiritual awakening.
  • Classical Mudras & Bandhas: Maha Mudra, Ashwini Mudra, Mula Bandha, and Uddiyana Bandha integration.
PILLAR 03 // 30 HOURS

Philosophy, Sutras & Yogic Ethics

30 Lecture Hours • Ancient Texts

Deep immersion into sacred texts. We examine Sage Patanjali's Yoga Sutras, the Bhagavad Gita's four paths of yoga, and the sacred ethical responsibilities of a certified teacher.

  • Patanjali's 8 Limbs: Systematic analysis of Yamas, Niyamas, Asana, Pranayama, Pratyahara, Dharana, Dhyana, Samadhi.
  • Bhagavad Gita Wisdom: The synthesis of Karma Yoga, Bhakti Yoga, Jnana Yoga, and Raja Yoga in modern life.
  • Parampara & Ethics: The sacred teacher-student covenant, ethical boundaries, and ashram decorum.
PILLAR 04 // 20 HOURS

Functional Anatomy & Physiology

20 Clinical Hours • Injury Prevention

Modern functional anatomy mapped directly to asana practice. Understand musculoskeletal kinematics, fascial lines, spine decompression, and injury avoidance.

  • Musculoskeletal Health: Joint mechanics, ligament safety, and lumbar/cervical spine protection.
  • Fascia & Nervous System: Vagus nerve stimulation, myofascial release, and parasympathetic activation.
  • Injury Protocols: Diagnostic screening, structural adjustments, and modification for physical limitations.
PILLAR 05 // 20 HOURS

Teaching Methodology & Practicum

20 Practicum Hours • Live Teaching Drills

Cultivate your authentic voice and classroom presence. Learn sequence architecture, hands-on adjustments, student modifications, and room energy management.

  • Verbal Cueing & Presence: Voice projection, spatial navigation, and clear biomechanical directives.
  • Sequence Architecture: Designing balanced 60, 75, and 90-minute therapeutic and vinyasa sequences.
  • Live Mock Practicum: Peer teaching evaluation, senior faculty feedback, and graduation certification drills.

06 // CLARITY & POLICIES

Frequently Asked Questions.

Essential clarity on certification credentials, prerequisite experience, campus logistics, and residential standards.

No previous advanced experience is necessary. The 40-Day Basic Level TTC is intentionally structured from the ground up. In Phase 01 (Shuddhi), our acharyas assess each student's skeletal alignment, joint mobility, and breath capacity, ensuring proper biomechanical foundations are anchored before advancing into complex vinyasa sequences and teaching practicum. Whether you are an earnest beginner seeking deep personal transformation or an experienced practitioner preparing for professional certification, you will be met at your individual threshold.

Upon satisfactory completion of all curriculum hours and practical evaluations, you are awarded the globally accredited Akshar Yoga Kendraa 200-Hour Residential Teacher Certificate. This credential meets the international standards recognized worldwide (including Yoga Alliance USA eligibility) and is aligned with the Yoga Certification Board (YCB), Government of India, enabling you to teach yoga professionally anywhere in the world.

CSE Residences (Tier 2) provides private 1-BHK serviced urban apartment suites within Bangalore, equipped with modern creature comforts, high-speed optical Wi-Fi, workspace, laundry, and daily dedicated air-conditioned transit to and from morning ashram shalas. It is ideal for seekers who desire private, quiet executive comfort alongside intensive sadhana.

Purantha Sanctuary (Tier 3) is an off-grid ecological nature sanctuary nestled on the forested outskirts of Bangalore. It offers open-air wooden shalas, on-campus Ayurvedic clinical therapies, medicinal herb showers, and continuous immersion in pristine silence and natural prana.

The ashram rhythm adheres strictly to classical Dinacharya, beginning at 05:30 AM with the Brahma Muhurta bell and concluding with lights out at 09:30 PM. Days comprise morning asana and pranayama (2h), nutritious Sattvik breakfast, academic lectures on anatomy and Patanjali sutras (2.5h), mindful lunch and rest, afternoon alignment practicum (2h), and evening chanting and meditation. Attendance of at least 95% is mandatory to ensure eligibility for graduation and accredited certificate issuance.

All residential packages include 100% pure Sattvik vegetarian meals prepared fresh according to classical Ayurvedic nutritional sciences. Dishes are prepared without garlic, onion, excessive oils, or artificial stimulants, utilizing seasonal organic produce, whole grains, and healing herbs. This dietary protocol purifies the digestive system, calms the mind, and optimizes energy levels throughout 40 days of intensive physical and spiritual practice. Vegan and food sensitivity requirements are accommodated with prior notice.

Kempegowda International Airport (BLR) serves Bangalore with extensive nonstop international and domestic flights. Both CSE Residences and Purantha Sanctuary are readily accessible from BLR (45 to 60 minutes). For all confirmed residential participants (Tiers 2 & 3), our admissions office coordinates dedicated chauffeur pickup and return airport transit upon submission of your flight itinerary.

ACADEMIC RIGOR // 200-HOUR SPECIFICATION

200-Hour Accredited Syllabus Structure

Certified by Yoga Alliance USA and Yoga Certification Board (YCB)

The 40-Day Basic Level TTC is an immersive residential course designed to awaken deep physical mastery and spiritual knowledge across five comprehensive disciplines:

1. Classical Asana Biomechanics (100h)
84 postures, standing stability, spinal decompression, backbends, and inversions.
2. Pranayama, Mudras & Energy Anatomy (30h)
Kumbhaka retention science, Nadi Shodhana, Bandhas, Chakras, and Kundalini mechanics.
3. Philosophy & Yogic Ethics (30h)
Patanjali's 8 Limbs, Bhagavad Gita synthesis, and ethical boundaries of a master teacher.
4. Functional Anatomy & Injury Prevention (20h)
Musculoskeletal biomechanics, fascial lines, and parasympathetic nervous activation.
5. Teaching Methodology & Practicum (20h)
Verbal cueing, safe hands-on assists, sequence architecture, and mock class evaluation.
